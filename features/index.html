01

One search,
seven sources

Type a song or an artist and Wispify searches Audius, JioSaavn, YouTube Music, Deezer, iTunes, Internet Archive and radio at once, and plays full-length tracks. Press / to jump to search.

The main screen stays calm: art, title, one play row and a small dock with Up next, Like, Volume and the Sound studio. Library, Discover, downloads, Save to folder, Copy link, Sleep timer and Player style all live in the ⋯ More menu. Synced lyrics can run under the player.

02

Change the song
while it plays

The Sound studio opens as a sheet over the player. Flip a preset and the song you're hearing changes on the spot: no re-download, no restart. Reverb costs nothing when it's off.

  • SlowedThe slowed version, live.
  • SpeedThe sped-up version.
  • NightcoreThe nightcore treatment.
  • ReverbA room around the track.
  • BassA low-end boost.
  • 0.75× – 1.5×Playback speed, and ±10 s jumps.

03

Up next,
on an orbit

Up next opens as a curved slider. Scroll, drag or use the arrow keys and it turns with a spring; the focused song grows, and a click or Enter plays it. The plain list (remove, clear) is one tap away.

Shuffle plays each song once per pass, Previous follows what you actually heard, and Repeat-one loops in place without fetching the stream again.

04

Shrink it
to a pill

The mini-player button in the title bar folds Wispify into a slim pill: cover, song, artist and play/pause. Close hides to the tray, and the music and voice keep running; quit from the tray.

Shown at about its real size.

05

reach through games

Global shortcuts work while another window has focus, and your keyboard's media keys and the Windows media flyout work too. Rebind any of them in Settings → Keys. Try one on this page.

07

Built for the
slow PC

Wispify was made for low-spec Windows machines first. These are the decisions that keep it out of your frame time.

  1. ≈0%CPU when idle. The mic is closed in push-to-talk, effects pause when the music does, and animations stop when the widget isn't focused.
  2. Hidden = stillThe widget stops painting while it's hidden or minimized.
  3. 1 canvasStage is a separate window, created when you open it and destroyed when you close it. Only the active template's visualizer is loaded; one canvas draw per frame, 60 fps cap, 30 on Low-impact.
  4. SSE4.2whisper.cpp is built for a portable baseline, so it runs on CPUs without AVX instead of crashing the first time you speak.
  5. Low-impactOne switch in Settings for older PCs: less CPU and battery, no glow, no spin.
  6. MicaNative Windows 11 Mica glass; solid on Windows 10. No backdrop blur anywhere.
