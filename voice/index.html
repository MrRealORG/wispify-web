Hold F8 anywhere in Windows, even with a fullscreen game focused, and say what you want. Your PC turns the speech into text; the grammar below turns the text into an action.

Settings → Voice

Three ways
to listen.

Default

Hold F8, speak, let go.

Zero CPU when idle; the mic is closed the rest of the time. The most forgiving mode: sound-alikes, near misses and bare song names all work, because the key press already says “this is for Wispify”.

Hands free

Say “Hey Wisp, next.”

An always-on, near-zero-CPU voice gate wakes Whisper only on speech, and only commands that start with the wake word run, so game voice chat can't skip your songs.

Quiet

No listening at all.

Hotkeys and everything else keep working. Change modes any time in Settings → Voice.

Under the hood

The model
lives on your PC

The first time you press Voice, Wispify downloads the selected Whisper model once, with progress. After that nothing you say leaves your computer. Base.en is the default: tiny mis-heard a clean test phrase, and song titles are exactly where that hurts. Small is there if your CPU is fast.

ModelDownloadOne command*
tiny.en31 MB≈0.15 sLow-impact
base.en57 MB≈0.39 sDefault
small.en181 MB≈1.5 sFast CPUs

*Encoder time for a 3–5 second command with Wispify's trimmed audio window, on a shared 2-vCPU test VM (±30%). From the project's voice model benchmark.
