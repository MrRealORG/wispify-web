Two ways to show it

Fullscreen,
or behind your icons.

Fullscreen
Takes the screen. Esc closes it.
Wallpaper
Sits behind the desktop icons as a live wallpaper, and repaints your normal wallpaper when it closes. Falls back to fullscreen if the desktop layer isn't available.
Lock
Makes the Stage click-through. Dim, Refresh and Close sit next to it.
Shortcut
Ctrl Alt V opens, unlocks or closes it. Rebindable.
Background
Blurred album art, a solid colour, or your own picture.
Customize
Per template: colour, visualizer, bar style, count and height, glow, lyrics on/off, size and position, title, clock, disc (art, vinyl, off) and spin, queue, background, blur and dim. Reset brings the preset back.
Lyrics
Synced lyrics from LRCLIB, word-timed when available and cached, with YouTube captions as a fallback. Hidden when a song has none.
