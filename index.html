What's inside

Small window.
Everything you reach for.

Everything that ships in 2.1.4, in the order you'll meet it. Hover a row to see the real screen.

  1. 01Hold F8. Say a song.Push-to-talk works while your game has focus. whisper.cpp turns speech into a command on your PC; the mic is closed the rest of the time.Voice
  2. 02Eight StagesA fullscreen or live-wallpaper visualizer: album-art disc, radial bars, synced lyrics, a clock and your queue. Eight templates, every one customizable.CtrlAltV
  3. 03One search, seven sourcesFull-length tracks from Audius, JioSaavn, YouTube Music, Deezer, iTunes, Internet Archive and radio. Offline downloads, liked songs and an orbit queue.Player
  4. 04Slowed. Reverb. Bass.Slowed, reverb, speed, nightcore and bass boost, changed live while the song keeps playing. No re-download, no restart.Sound studio
  5. 05The pillCollapse the player to a slim pill: cover, song, play/pause. It stays out of the way of whatever you're doing.Mini player
  6. 06Hotkeys that reach through gamesGlobal shortcuts for play, skip, like, volume, effects and Stage, plus your hardware media keys. Rebind any of them.CtrlAltP
  7. 07Next to nothing at idleAbout 0% CPU when idle. Effects pause when the music does, animations stop when the widget isn't focused, Stage is capped at 60 fps.Lightweight
  8. 08What you say stays hereThe voice model runs locally. In wake-word mode only commands that start with "Hey Wisp" run, so game chat can't skip your songs.Private

The details

Made for the
slow PC first.

Written, designed and built by MrReal / Ahmad Raza, for low-spec PCs first, because that's where a music app shouldn't cost you frames.

Format
Windows 10 & 11, x64. Installer or portable exe.
Runtime
Tauri 2 on WebView2, which is built into Windows.
Voice
whisper.cpp with Whisper base.en (≈57 MB), downloaded once on your first Voice press.
Idle
≈0% CPU. Mic closed in push-to-talk.
Stage
One canvas draw per frame, 60 fps cap, 30 on Low-impact.
CPU floor
Any x86-64 with SSE4.2. No AVX required.
Window
Frameless, solid by default; Windows 11 Mica glass if you want it.
Licence
MIT. Free.
