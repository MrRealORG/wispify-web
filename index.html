Liner notes

Pressing
details

Written, designed and built by MrReal / Ahmad Raza. For low-spec PCs first, because that's where a music app shouldn't cost you frames.

Format
Windows 10 & 11, x64. Installer or portable exe.
Runtime
Tauri 2 on WebView2, which is built into Windows.
Voice
whisper.cpp with Whisper base.en (≈57 MB), downloaded once on your first Voice press.
Idle
≈0% CPU. Mic closed in push-to-talk.
Stage
One canvas draw per frame, 60 fps cap, 30 on Low-impact.
CPU floor
Any x86-64 with SSE4.2. No AVX required.
Licence
MIT. Free.
