What it does

Small enough to forget.
Good enough to keep open.

Player

Full-length tracks from many sources, in one search.

Search once and Wispify looks across its free music sources at the same time. Download songs for offline listening, keep a Liked list and build playlists.

Sound studio

Slowed, reverb, speed and bass, live.

Change how a song sounds while it plays. No re-download, no restart.

Orbit queue

Up next, on a curve.

Scroll, drag or use arrow keys to turn the queue. The focused song grows; click or press Enter to play it.

Hotkeys

Global and rebindable.

Play, skip, like, volume, effects and Stage from anywhere. Change any of them in Settings → Keys.

Lightweight

Built for low-spec PCs.

The mic stays closed in push-to-talk, effects pause when music does, and animations stop when the widget isn’t focused.

Voice

Say it. Don’t alt-tab.

Hold F8 and speak, or say “Hey Wisp”. It works while your game has focus. Speech is recognised on your own PC with whisper.cpp, so it’s offline and private.

Stage

Turn your desktop into the music.

A fullscreen or live-wallpaper visualizer with audio-reactive bars, synced lyrics, a clock and your queue. Press Ctrl+Alt+V to open, unlock or close it.

Two modes

Fullscreen, or Wallpaper, where the Stage sits behind your desktop icons. Lock makes it click-through.

Make it yours

Every template is customizable: colour, visualizer, bars, lyrics, clock, disc, queue slider and background. Saved per template.

Light by design

Created when you open it and destroyed when you close it. One canvas draw per frame, capped at 60 fps, and it stops when paused.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Wispify free?

Yes. Wispify is free and open source under the MIT license.

Does voice control send my audio anywhere?

No. Speech is recognised on your PC with whisper.cpp. In push-to-talk mode the mic is only open while you hold the key.

Will it work while I’m in a game?

Yes. Push-to-talk, the wake word and the global hotkeys all work while another app has focus. A small overlay toast confirms what happened without stealing focus, and an optional chime covers exclusive-fullscreen games.

Where does the music come from?

One search covers Audius, JioSaavn, YouTube Music, Deezer, iTunes, Internet Archive and radio. You can also download songs to listen offline.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11 (x64) with WebView2, which is built into Windows. Any x86-64 CPU with SSE4.2 works.

Is there a version that doesn’t need installing?

Yes. Each release ships a portable exe alongside the installer.

What is the Stage?

A fullscreen or live-wallpaper visualizer with audio-reactive bars, synced lyrics, a clock and your queue. Pick one of 8 templates and customize it. Open it with Ctrl+Alt+V.

Can I change the shortcuts?

Every global shortcut can be rebound in Settings → Keys.
