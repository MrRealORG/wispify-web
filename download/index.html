First run

  1. 1

    Run the installer, or the portable exe.

  2. 2

    Search a song and press play. That's the player.

  3. 3

    Press Voice once. Whisper base.en downloads with a progress bar.

  4. 4

    Hold F8, say “play” and a song. Let go.

  5. 5

    Press Ctrl Alt V for the Stage.

Release notes

What's new
in 2.1.

Full changelog →

2.1.4Smoother YouTube fallback, sharper Stage

Songs played through YouTube's official player leave the loading state and pause properly. The Stage background is sharper (real blur at half resolution; your own picture stays unblurred) and covers load at 1200 px.

2.1.3Fewer skipped songs

YouTube songs that resolve but won't play fall back to the official player instead of being skipped. The wallpaper Stage keeps its desktop layer on Windows 11 24H2.

2.1.2YouTube and Stage fixes

YouTube no longer skips every song, downloads work again, and the Stage opens reliably.

2.1.1One seek bar, smarter voice and lyrics

A simpler compact view and grouped Settings. Voice got cleaner audio processing, beam search and the forgiving push-to-talk grammar (sound-alikes, near misses, bare song names) plus speed, nightcore and bass commands. Effects now work on every source, and lyrics are found for more songs.

2.1.0Stage, the orbit queue, a calmer player

The Stage visualizer with 8 templates, the orbit queue, the ⋯ More menu, a new voice screen, media keys, the new icon, and a long list of shuffle and repeat fixes.
